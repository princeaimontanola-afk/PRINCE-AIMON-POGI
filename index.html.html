About

Editing is where the real story gets found.

Prince Aimon is a photo and video editor who treats post-production as the second half of the shoot, not an afterthought. Over years of working across weddings, brand campaigns, music videos and live events, he's built a style that favours mood and rhythm over flashy effects.

Every project starts with the footage as it actually is — the light that was available, the moment that was caught by accident — and gets shaped from there: color graded for feeling, retouched with restraint, cut for pace. The goal is always the same: the final piece should feel inevitable, like it couldn't look any other way.

  • ◆Advanced photo retouching & compositing
  • ◆Narrative-driven video editing
  • ◆Cinematic color grading
  • ◆Fast turnaround for events & social
